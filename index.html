MyHealth

A private personal tool for one household.

MyHealth is a small script that reads the owner's and their family members' own Fitbit / Google Health data (activity, heart rate, sleep) once a day, with each person's explicit consent, and copies a daily summary into the owner's private Google Sheet for personal review.

It is not a public service, has no users outside the owner's household, and does not sell, share or advertise with any data.
