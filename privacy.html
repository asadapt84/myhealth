MyHealth Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Who uses this app

MyHealth is a personal, non-commercial tool used only by its owner and members of the owner's household. Each person signs in with their own Google account and explicitly grants access.

What data is accessed

With read-only Google Health API permissions, MyHealth reads the signed-in person's activity and fitness data (steps, distance, floors, calories, active minutes, workouts), health metrics (heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, weight, body fat), sleep data, and nutrition data (logged foods, meals, calories, nutrients and water intake). With the Google Drive "drive.file" permission it can create and edit only the single spreadsheet it creates itself; it cannot see any other files.

How the data is used

Data is used only to produce a daily summary for the person it belongs to and their household. It is stored on the owner's personal computer and in the owner's private Google Sheet. It may be reviewed with an AI assistant chosen by the owner for personal health insights.

Sharing

Data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with third parties other than as described above. MyHealth's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept until the owner deletes it. Anyone can stop access at any time by removing MyHealth at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can ask for their stored data to be deleted by emailing the contact below.
